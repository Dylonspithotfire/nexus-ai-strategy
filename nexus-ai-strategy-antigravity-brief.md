# Nexus AI Strategy — Live Landing Page Brief
## For building with Google Antigravity

**Project:** A live, single-page landing page for Nexus AI Strategy that Dylan both designed and built.
**Portfolio angle:** This is the design-to-code piece — most brand designers can't ship code, most devs can't design. One live URL proves both, plus conversion thinking for ads/client work.

## 1. The client

**Nexus AI Strategy** — a solo consultancy founded by Dylan Curry.
**Positioning:** "Fractional AI Architect" for mid-sized logistics and supply chain firms.
**Rule of One:** One specialist, one niche, one problem.

**Target firms:** $10M–$50M revenue, 50–200 employees, logistics / supply chain.
**Target people:** Operations executives and owners, roughly 40s–60s. Non-technical decision-makers who are skeptical of AI salespeople, but respect operators who speak their language. They care about whether this person can walk into their operation and find money — not AI hype.

**Brand personality:** Precise. Confident. Pragmatic. No fluff.

**Brand constraints (carried over from the Nexus brand identity brief):**
- Must read well in single color — these clients still print things.
- No generic AI clichés: no neon gradients, no robot heads, no circuit-board textures.
- Should feel like it belongs in a boardroom, not a hackathon.
- A logistics owner glancing at it should think "this person looks expensive and competent."

**Logo / palette / type note:** No final Nexus palette or type pairing is on record yet. If your brand identity work is finalized, use that system everywhere in this build and ignore the proposed direction below. If not, use this proposed direction as a starting point and swap it later — do not present it as already-decided brand work:
- Proposed palette: Deep navy `#10243E`, slate `#5A6B7F`, paper/off-white `#F6F4EF`, muted copper accent `#C98A2E`
- Proposed type: one bold sans for display (e.g. Archivo / Inter Tight), one clean sans for body (e.g. Inter)
- Keep contrast high, layout spacious, and imagery grounded — warehouse / operations / people at work, not sci-fi stock.

## 4. Page structure and copy

### Navigation
Nexus AI Strategy [logo] | Services | Process | Audit Quiz | FAQ | **Book the Audit** button

### Hero
- Eyebrow: Fractional AI Architect — for logistics and supply chain
- Headline: AI that finds money in your operation. Not hype in your inbox.
- Subhead: I help mid-sized logistics firms find the one or two places AI actually pays off — then build it, measure it, and move on to the next bottleneck.
- Primary CTA: Book the $3,500 AI Readiness Audit
- Secondary CTA: Take the 2-minute audit quiz
- Small trust line: For firms doing $10M–$50M, with 50–200 employees. Solo consultancy — you work directly with me, not a junior team.

### Who this is for / who it isn't for
- **For:** Logistics and supply chain operators who know something in dispatch, inventory, warehousing, or customer ops is costing them time and money, but don't know where AI fits.
- **Not for:** Companies looking for a generic chatbot, a vendor demo, or AI for its own sake.

### The problem
AI vendors sell tools. Operators need outcomes. Most firms don't have an AI problem — they have a bottleneck problem, a data problem, or a process problem that AI might solve, or might not. The Audit exists to tell the truth about that before anyone spends $15K building something.

### Services
Three cards, using the pricing and descriptions in Section 3. Make the Audit card visually primary. Each card ends with the same action: "Start with the Audit" for the Audit card, and "Book a call" for the other two.

### How it works — 4 steps
1. **Audit (Week 1):** Walkthrough, systems/data review, ROI-ranked roadmap.
2. **Decide:** You keep the roadmap either way. No obligation to build.
3. **Build:** One scoped implementation, with a clear definition of done.
4. **Measure and iterate:** Retainer clients get ongoing prioritization and improvement.

### Audit intake quiz (interactive — required)
A short quiz, 5 questions, with a result at the end:
1. Company size band: Under 50 / 50–200 / Over 200 employees
2. Biggest bottleneck right now: Dispatch & routing / Inventory / Warehouse ops / Customer service & quoting / Something else
3. What tools do you run on today? [short text answer]
4. How clean / accessible is your operational data? Messy / Mostly usable / Clean and centralized
5. Timeline: Just exploring / This quarter / Need help now

**Result logic (keep it simple and honest):**
- Most answers point to "Start with the Audit" — that's the correct entry point for almost everyone.
- If they pick "Need help now" + a clear bottleneck + usable data, the result can say: "Audit first, with implementation as the likely next step — book a call to scope it."
- Never promise a specific saving, ROI figure, or outcome. The quiz recommends a next step, not a quote.

End every result with: name, company, work email, and "Request the Audit" button.

### Simple ROI check (stretch goal — include only if it doesn't bloat the page)
A tiny calculator, not a sales claim:
- Hours saved per week [input] × loaded hourly rate [input] × 52 = estimated annual value of that time
- Label it clearly as an estimate based on their own inputs, not a guaranteed result.

### About
Keep this short and honest — do not claim a completed degree or client results you don't have yet:
> Nexus AI Strategy is a solo consultancy run by Dylan Curry. I design and build AI workflows for logistics operations, and I bring the same discipline to client work that I bring to my own builds: scope it tight, ship it, measure it.

[Confirm / edit this bio before publishing. Do not add credentials, clients, or results that aren't verified.]

### FAQ (answer in one or two sentences each)
1. Do I need AI tools already? No — the Audit starts with your current operation, not a tool purchase.
2. How long does the Audit take? Scope it as a one-week engagement in the copy, and confirm the timeline on the call.
3. Will AI replace my staff? No — the goal is to remove repetitive work so your team can handle the work that actually needs people.
4. What if the Audit finds AI won't help much? Then you keep a clear roadmap and you haven't spent implementation money finding that out.
5. Why a solo consultant instead of a firm? You work directly with the person doing the thinking and the building — no handoffs.

### Final CTA + contact form
- Headline: Find out where AI actually pays off in your operation.
- Fields: Name, Company, Work email, Company size band (dropdown), Biggest bottleneck (dropdown), Message (optional)
- On submit: show a clear confirmation — "Thanks, I'll reply within [X business days]" — and do not show a fake booking confirmation.
- Contact details to confirm before publishing: [booking link, if any] / [contact email — confirm before publishing; do not publish a personal inbox without deciding it is the business contact]

## 5. What to tell Antigravity to build

**Build:** One responsive, mobile-first, single-page static site (HTML/CSS/JS). No backend, no payment, no database. The quiz and calculator run in the browser. The form shows validation and a confirmation state.

**Use Antigravity's parallel agents:**
- Agent 1: Build the page structure, copy, and styling.
- Agent 2: Build and test the quiz logic, result states, and calculator math.
- Agent 3: Test it — mobile (375px), tablet, desktop, keyboard navigation, all links and CTAs, form validation, and browser verification with screenshots.

**Verification checklist before you call it done:**
- Every button and link goes somewhere real on the page — no dead CTAs.
- No lorem ipsum, no placeholder images, no neon gradients or robot/circuit clichés.
- Form validates email and required fields, and shows a confirmation on submit.
- Quiz always ends in a recommendation + request step, never a dead end.
- Text contrast is readable, headings are semantic, images have alt text.
- Page reads cleanly in single color / printed — check the hero and services cards in grayscale.
