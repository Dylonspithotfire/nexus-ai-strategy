Operational Friction

The margin leaks hiding in your daily operations.

In mid-sized freight and supply chain operations, margin doesn't vanish in large executive errors. It bleeds out in hundreds of daily manual micro-tasks between legacy systems and frantic email chains.

01

Carrier Invoice & Accessorial Leakage

12% to 18% of carrier invoices carry rate discrepancies, unvalidated detention charges, or mismatched fuel tables. Because AP clerks only have 90 seconds per invoice, unverified fees get rubber-stamped straight onto your P&L.

Typical Impact: $45,000–$180,000/yr in unrecovered billing leakage
02

The "Where's My Load?" Email Firefight

Customer support reps and dispatchers burn 3 to 4 hours every single day reading unstructured emails, logging into carrier portals, matching container IDs, and re-typing ETA updates into customer portals.

Typical Impact: 30–50% of dispatcher bandwidth trapped in manual status inquiries
03

Legacy Stack Isolation & PDF Rekeying

Your core TMS (McLeod, TMW, proprietary SQL) works, but it's an island. When rate cons, bills of lading (BOL), and delivery receipts arrive as blurry PDFs or messy spreadsheets, your staff has to re-key every line item manually.

Typical Impact: 5–8% rate of manual transcription errors causing dock delays
04

The Enterprise Consulting Trap

Big consultancies quote $250,000 for an 8-month "AI strategy assessment" that ends in a slide deck. Meanwhile, SaaS point-solutions try to force you into rigid workflows that break your competitive advantages.

Typical Impact: Months wasted, massive shelfware fees, zero deployed agents

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, transparent responses to common operational, technical, and commercial concerns.

How do you integrate with our legacy TMS (McLeod, TMW, MercuryGate, on-prem SQL)? +

We do not require you to rip and replace your core system. Depending on your infrastructure, we integrate via:

  • Direct read-only SQL database replicas or staging tables for load and billing data.
  • Automated SFTP batch drops (CSV, EDI 210, 214, 204 feeds).
  • REST/SOAP web services where available in your TMS version.
  • Secure email queue ingestion with human-in-the-loop validation dashboards.

During the $3,500 Readiness Audit, we thoroughly review your database access and API capabilities to ensure zero disruption to live dispatch operations.

Is our proprietary freight pricing, customer lists, and margin data kept private? +

Yes, completely. We enforce enterprise-grade data containment standards:

  • Zero public model training: Your proprietary data is never used to train public LLM models.
  • Private cloud tenant architecture: All agent pipelines run in dedicated, isolated cloud tenants (AWS or Google Cloud) under your company's ownership.
  • Strict data sanitation: Personally identifiable customer information and sensitive margin parameters are tokenized or masked prior to LLM inference.
Why hire a Fractional AI Architect instead of a full-time in-house AI engineer? +

A qualified senior AI architect demands $220,000–$300,000 in base salary, plus benefits, equity, and recruiting fees. Furthermore, a mid-sized logistics firm ($10M–$50M) rarely needs 40 hours a week of permanent architecture once core agents are deployed.

With Nexus, you get elite enterprise architecture expertise on a fixed-scope audit ($3,500) or flexible monthly advisory ($4,000/mo) without permanent executive overhead or equity dilution.

What happens if an AI agent makes a mistake on an invoice or dispatch record? +

We build deterministic validation guardrails into every agent pipeline. AI agents are never allowed to execute irreversible financial or dispatch operations autonomously without passing strict mathematical checks.

If an invoice has a discrepancy exceeding your pre-set tolerance (e.g., >$25 or missing signed POD), the agent routes it directly to a clean human review queue with the relevant contract clause highlighted. Your staff retains 100% decision authority.

What is the timeline from the $3,500 Readiness Audit to live deployment? +

The AI Readiness Audit takes exactly 10 business days from kick-off to delivery of the Boardroom Executive Decision Memo.

If you choose to proceed with an Agent Implementation ($15,000), engineering and initial sandbox testing typically take 3 to 5 weeks, followed by 2 weeks of parallel run testing with your operations team before full cutover.

Why don't you guarantee a specific dollar ROI upfront? +

Because any consultant who promises a 10x ROI before inspecting your actual database, carrier contracts, and error rates is guessing.

In supply chain operations, ROI depends on baseline volume, rate-con consistency, and legacy software access. We provide realistic, mathematically defensible estimates based on your own team's numbers, then prove exact figures during the audit. Boardroom credibility demands honesty over sales gimmicks.
